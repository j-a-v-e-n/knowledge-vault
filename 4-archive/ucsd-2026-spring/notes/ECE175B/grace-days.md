## ECE175B Late Policy（来自 lecture-1a syllabus）

- **0–24h late** = 75% credit
- **24–48h late** = 50% credit
- **>48h late** = 0% credit
- **Grace days**: 5 total for the quarter, applied first before late penalties kick in
- **Grace day 用法**：notify TA via email；提交时间可以推 24h（每用 1 个 grace day = 24h 不扣分）
