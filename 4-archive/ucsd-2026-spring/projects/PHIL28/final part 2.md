### E1 英文成稿（约 430 词，背这个）
1. Fish and Shiffrin defend opposing views on free speech and its value. Outline the
main differences between them and indicate which view is superior

Fish and Shiffrin hold opposite views about free speech. They disagree on three main points.

**1. Does free speech have value in itself?** Shiffrin says yes. For her, free speech has a deep and basic value. We are thinking beings. To be a full person, we must be able to put our thoughts into words — to know ourselves, to be known by others, and to share a moral life with others. Without speech, the mind is trapped, like a prison inside the head. So free speech matters for its own sake. Fish says no. For him, "free speech" is an empty label. It has no fixed content of its own. People fill it with whatever cause they want to push. So free speech is not a value in itself. It is a prize that each side fights to claim.

**2. Can speech be separated from action?** Shiffrin says speech is special and unlike action. With words, you can test an idea, say "maybe," and revise it or take it back later. A punch cannot be thrown with a "maybe." So speech has a special, workshop-like role that action does not. Fish says there is no "pure" speech that stands apart from action. Every word has effects in the world. An insult, for example, hurts the moment it is spoken, and no later words can fully take that harm back. So there is no clean zone of harmless speech for the law to protect.

**3. Can the law hold a neutral, principled rule for free speech?** Shiffrin says yes. Her thinker-based principle can give principled answers about which speech to protect and which to regulate. Fish says no. Any rule must draw a line, and every line favors one side over another. So the line is always a political choice that only pretends to be neutral.

**Which view is superior?** Fish's view is stronger. Shiffrin does not really stay at the level of principle. She decides case by case — business speech, children's speech, and so on. She even admits her theory cannot do everything, and that other values must help decide the hard cases. This is exactly Fish's point. When we actually draw the lines, we are making political choices, not applying one clean principle. Her own practice proves him right. Still, Shiffrin is right about one thing. She explains better why we protect private diaries, art, and personal talk, which serve no public cause. But that win is at the level of the ideal. At the level of law, Fish is more honest: there is no neutral ground.

### 中文对照（理解用，不用背）

Fish 和 Shiffrin 对言论自由持相反观点。他们在三个主要点上分歧。

**1. 言论自由本身有价值吗？** Shiffrin 说有。对她来说，言论自由有一种深层、根本的价值。我们是会思考的存在。要成为一个完整的人，我们必须能把想法说出来——认识自己、被他人认识、与他人共享道德生活。没有言论，心灵就被困住，像在自己脑袋里的一种单独监禁。所以言论自由本身就值得珍视。 Fish 说没有。对他来说，"言论自由"是个空标签，本身没有固定内容。人们想推进什么主张，就往里填什么。所以言论自由本身不是价值，而是各方争着去抢的一个奖品。

**2. 言论能和行动分开吗？** Shiffrin 说言论很特殊、和行动不同。用语言，你可以试探一个想法、说"也许"、之后再修改或收回。可一拳没法带着"也许"打出去。所以言论有一种行动没有的、像工作坊一样的特殊角色。 Fish 说不存在与行动分离的"纯言论"。每句话在世界上都有后果。比如一句侮辱，说出口的那一刻就造成伤害，后面再多的话也无法完全收回。所以根本没有一块"无害言论"的干净地带供法律去保护。

**3. 法律能不能守住一条中立、有原则的言论自由规则？** Shiffrin 说能。她的"思想者"原理能就"哪些言论该保护、哪些该管制"给出有原则的答案。 Fish 说不能。任何规则都得划线，而每条线都偏袒一方。所以那条线永远是个政治选择，只是假装中立。

**哪种观点更优？** Fish 更强。Shiffrin 其实并没有停在原则层面。她是一个案子一个案子地裁——商业言论、儿童言论等等。她甚至承认自己的理论不能搞定一切、必须靠其他价值来帮忙定那些难案。这恰恰是 Fish 的观点：当我们真的去划线时，做的是政治选择，而不是套用一条干净的原则。她自己的做法证明了他对。 不过 Shiffrin 有一点是对的。她更好地解释了我们为什么保护私人日记、艺术和个人闲谈——这些并不服务任何公共事业。但那个胜利是在"理想"层面。在"法律"层面，Fish 更诚实：没有中立的立场。
