# Public-page factual audit

Status: local draft; not sent or published.

Target reviewed: <https://saffronorestaurant.com/> and its public menu page, captured on 2026-07-27.

## Directly observed

- The homepage identifies Saffrono Lounge Bar & Grill with Persian and Afghani cuisine in Del Mar, San Diego.
- The same homepage contains repeated generic burger entries, pizza content, generic prices, and template placeholder text.
- The public menu page contains recognizable Persian and Afghan dishes alongside repeated generic burger and placeholder sections.
- The site already links to menu, ordering, reservation, catering, social, and review destinations.

## Unknown

- Who currently controls the domain and content system.
- Whether the business owner knows about or accepts the template content.
- Whether any menu item, description, price, opening status, link, or location statement is current.
- Whether customers use the website enough for a repair to affect behavior.
- Whether the business is already paying someone to change it.
- Whether the authorized decision-maker would value or buy a repair.

## Bounded repair hypothesis

Remove unrelated template content and reorganize only owner-verified facts into a clear homepage with direct paths to menu and catering. Preserve existing external systems instead of replacing ordering, booking, or hosting during the first repair.

## Required owner verification before publication

- Restaurant name, cuisine description, location wording, operating status, and contact details;
- current menu source of truth;
- approved dish names, descriptions, prices, photography, logo, colors, and links;
- content ownership and permission to reuse any existing assets;
- domain, hosting, maintenance, analytics, privacy, and accessibility requirements.

## What this audit does not establish

It does not establish lost revenue, customer conversion, owner dissatisfaction, budget, decision authority, technical access, or willingness to pay.
