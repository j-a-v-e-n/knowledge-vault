## Verdict

**BLOCKED.** V1 remains a useful problem statement and candidate-backlog item,
but it is not executable enough to govern stage closure. It must not be
promoted, used to freeze a stage, or used to change completion semantics.

Repeated discovery of the same blocking surfaces is not itself convergence or
acceptance. A later design must be a smaller successor artifact rather than an
edit to V1.

## Critical findings

### SCV1-CRIT-FINDING-CONTINUITY

Finding renames, splits, deletion, severity downgrade, and conversion to an
unmonitored unknown can manufacture an apparently empty Critical/Major set.
V1 has no authoritative finding history, split/alias/supersession lineage, or
severity-change authority. Its deferrable-Minor language also conflicts with
the current stricter freeze contract.

Minimum successor requirement: append-only finding history with stable IDs,
parent/child and supersession edges, full severity history, authorized change
records, and machine-recomputed open state. A material, unmonitored, expired,
or failed-trigger unknown remains blocking.

### SCV1-CRIT-SUBJECT-CLOSURE-IDENTITY

V1 treats any later modification as a new candidate, but current governance
must append review evidence after fixing the reviewed subject. If the subject
and its closure evidence share one candidate tree, every review creates a new
tree that cannot inherit the prior verdict; reviewing that tree creates the
next tree. The identity model therefore recurses without a terminal point.

Minimum successor requirement: reuse three distinct identities—immutable
subject C, append-only closure evidence B, and bundle D that binds C and B.
Only a change inside the declared subject boundary creates a new candidate;
authorized evidence append does not mutate the subject.

### SCV1-CRIT-CLAIM-CEILING

Natural-language disclaimers do not prevent a consumer from mapping a generic
`eligible` result to a stronger release, usability, or financial claim. V1 has
no exact stage identity, allowed output transition, or machine-enforced claim
ceiling.

Minimum successor requirement: bind the current `stage_id`, allowed output
states, explicit `must_not_claim`, and the existing product-state model.
Convergence may stop additional review; it cannot independently produce pass,
freeze, product usability, or financial-effectiveness status.

## Major findings

### SCV1-MAJOR-FALSE-CONVERGENCE

Same-origin reviewers, shared evidence lineage, fixed prompts, reviewer
fatigue, and no-verdict runs can imitate convergence. V1 has no falsifiable
stopping predicate or lineage-equivalence rule.

Minimum successor requirement: preregister bounded attack families and review
budget; group reviewers and evidence by lineage; count no-verdict as no
support. Budget exhaustion yields `bounded_incomplete_not_passed`, never pass.

### SCV1-MAJOR-TYPE-INAPPROPRIATE-CLOSURE

The reproduce → root cause → repair → negative regression → adjacent positive
path chain is suitable for many behavioral code defects but not universally
for authority, provenance, semantic/source, non-reproducible external, or
prospective longitudinal findings.

Minimum successor requirement: retain a common falsification/root-cause/
corrective-disposition core, then select a declared evidence profile by finding
type. A builder may not self-authorize `not applicable` for a required link.

### SCV1-MAJOR-SCOPE-ORPHANS

Asking a reviewer to challenge scope does not establish the authoritative set
against which omissions can be detected. A missing high-impact requirement,
failure class, implementation target, real entry point, or controlled file can
remain outside every declared finding.

Minimum successor requirement: derive a scope manifest from the existing
charter and frozen contract, record exclusions and their claim ceilings, and
block on any high-impact orphan or missing review target.

### SCV1-MAJOR-EFFECT-SEMANTICS

V1 correctly has no normative authority: no policy or verifier consumes its
proposed closure semantics. It is not operationally inert, however. The current
foundation packet controls the whole `audits/execution_loop_v2` tree; V1 also
appears in attempt evidence and participates in file-hash and freshness checks.
Narrative churn therefore creates real ledger/freshness maintenance cost.

Minimum successor requirement: distinguish `normative_effect` from explicit
operational dependencies. Bind immutable review artifacts individually rather
than treating unrelated narrative changes across an audit tree as stopping or
defect-resolution progress.

The current invalid execution status also includes an interrupted operation
and stale whole-tree snapshot. It must not be attributed solely to V1; the

## Minimum executable successor

A future V2 design may be considered only if it is smaller than this narrative
proposal and mechanically provides:

- distinct subject C, closure B, and bundle D identities;
- exact stage and claim ceiling;
- append-only finding continuity and authorized severity transitions;
- type-appropriate closure profiles;
- executable unknown detectors, expiry, reopen events, and failed-trigger
  dispositions;
- a contract-derived scope manifest with orphan detection;
- evidence-lineage grouping, novelty probes, and no-verdict handling;
- a bounded stopping predicate whose only positive power is to stop further
  review, not to create acceptance;
- separate normative-effect and operational-dependency fields.

Assurance cases, independent verification and validation, and
defeater/eliminative argumentation remain useful design traditions and review
analogies. They do not automatically authorize this candidate or its successor.
