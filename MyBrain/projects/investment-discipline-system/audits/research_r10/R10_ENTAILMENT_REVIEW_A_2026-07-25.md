## R10-MECH-SELF-PREFERENCE

- Severity: `major`
- State: `open`
- Claim location: failure graph and failure-class table

The self-preference paper studies a judge model's preference for answers
generated by its own model and separates answer quality with gold judgments.
It does not study a software builder authoring the test oracle. SpecBench
studies optimization against visible tests and the held-out gap. The candidate
incorrectly merged identity preference, shared blind spots, proxy optimization,
and circular test generation into one mechanism.

Required repair:

- separate same-model evaluator identity bias;
- separate builder-controlled oracle/proxy optimization;
- separate shared-source blind spots;
- mark circular-oracle risk as an engineering hypothesis unless direct evidence
  is supplied.
