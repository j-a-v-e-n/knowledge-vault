## BS/MS Course Transfer 机制（**verified 2026-05-14**）

⚠️ **没有"双算" / double-counting**。UCSD ECE 政策明确禁止。来源：[ECE BS Students Admitted to MS Program](https://ece.ucsd.edu/graduate/ece-bs-students-admitted-ms-program) + [BSMS FAQ](https://www.ece.ucsd.edu/graduate/bsms-frequently-asked-questions-page)（2026-05-14 fetched）。

**真实机制 = Transfer**：

| 项 | 政策 |
|---|---|
| Double counting | ❌ 禁止 |
| Transfer cap | **24 units (6 courses)** from BS record to MS |
| Eligible courses | 必须在 BS audit "Elective Courses" section（**不**算作 major/college 任何要求） |
| Grade | letter grade, **B- 或以上** |
| Grades transfer | ❌ 只 transfer units，不 transfer 成绩 |
| Sequence | BS 必须先 confer，再 initiate transfer |
| Process | Email ECE MS Academic Advising + PDF degree audit highlight + GEPA ePetition |

**操作含义**：

- Senior year 上 grad ECE 课时，**不能**用它填 BS TE / Breadth / Design 槽
- 这门课就停留在 audit 末尾的 "Elective Courses" section（跟现有的 CSE 8A / VIS 9 等并列）
- BS 毕业后，从这些 elective grad 课里挑最多 6 门 transfer 到 MS

**对 MS 减负效果**：

- MS Plan II (comp exam): **48 units = 12 课**（[MS Degree Requirements](https://ece.ucsd.edu/graduate/ms-degree-requirements)）
- Plan I (thesis): 52 units
- 24 units transfer → MS 阶段实际只需再上 **24 units = 6 门**（Plan II）
- 注：Plan II 要求至少 **16 units (4 门) 是 201+ 级 ECE 课**（grad-level）

**适合 transfer 的 grad ECE 候选**：
- ECE 269 — Linear Algebra & Applications
- ECE 271A — Statistical Learning I
- ECE 272A — Stochastic Processes in Dynamic Systems
- ECE 276A — Sensing & Estimation in Robotics
- ECE 276B — Planning & Learning in Robotics
- ECE 271B — Statistical Learning II

**Senior year 上 grad 课的额外要求**：通过 **EASy 申请**（TritonLink）+ 系里 approval 才能注册 grad 课。

### 重叠规则 (verified 2026-05-15)

**AHI ∩ DEI（同一门课双算）**：UCSD catalog + Senate + Warren u-reqs 页面均**未明确说 yes/no**。DEI 政策 (Warren u-reqs.html) 写 "This course may overlap with the major, minor, or general education requirements"——**未列 AHI**。AHI 是 university requirement 不是 GE，所以 DEI 的明文允许范围没 cover AHI。**实务上**：UCSD 学生常用 ETHN 1 / HILD 7B / HILD 7C 等出现在 AHI + DEI **两个 approved list 上**的课同时打两个勾——但**没书面背书**。**操作**：advisor 当面 confirm + 选课时确认该课同时出现在两 list。

**TE ∩ Warren Area Study**：**禁止**。Warren GE 页 (warren.ucsd.edu/academics/general-education/area-studies.html) 明确："Upper-division major or minor courses may not overlap with Area Study or other general education requirements." → 一门 COGS UD **不能**同时算 ECE TE + Warren SS Area Study，只能算一个。

**Formal Skills ∩ major**：**允许重叠**（Warren GE 页"Formal Skills"section 明文 "Courses may overlap with your major, minor, or other general education requirements"）→ MATH 109 既算 TE 又算 Formal Skills 是 OK 的。
