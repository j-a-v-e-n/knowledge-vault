QUESTION 1
According to Piaget, why is knowledge not simply “found” in the world?

A. The mind uses logic-like operations to construct knowledge and test it through action.
B. Children copy their knowledge directly from adults.
C. Knowledge develops only through language.
D. The world is unknowable.

Correct answer: A
Answer-key explanation: Piaget treats knowledge as a construction produced by the mind’s organized operations and checked in action.

QUESTION 2
What is the zone of proximal development?

A. The difference between a learner’s memory and attention.
B. The gap between what a learner can do independently and what they can do with help from a knowledgeable other.
C. A fixed stage that every child reaches at the same age.
D. A method for memorizing cultural facts.

Correct answer: B
Answer-key explanation: The ZPD identifies what becomes possible when assistance is appropriately matched to the learner.

QUESTION 3
What does internalization mean in Vygotsky’s theory?

A. Memorizing an answer without interacting with others.
B. Replacing language with formal logic.
C. Moving culturally shaped meanings from social interaction into individual thought.
D. Ignoring culture in order to think independently.

Correct answer: C
Answer-key explanation: Vygotsky sees social interaction and speech as the source of meanings that become tools of individual thought.
