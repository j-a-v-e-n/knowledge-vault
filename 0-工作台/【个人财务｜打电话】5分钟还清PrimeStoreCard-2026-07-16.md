## 拨通后照念（对方问啥答啥即可）

1. 开场一句话说清来意：
   > **"Hi, I opened an Amazon Prime Store Card in mid-June but never received the physical card or any statement. I'd like to verify my identity, pay my balance in full, and get a replacement card."**
2. 验身份：报 SSN / 生日 / 地址。
3. 听余额——应该在 **$525.61 上下**（我从 Amazon 交易记录逐笔加总、与 5% 返点 $26.28 精确互证）。如果多出二三十刀，问一句是不是 late fee，然后要求减免：
   > **"Since I never received the card or statement, could you waive that as a one-time courtesy?"**
4. 付清：
   > **"I'd like to pay the balance in full today with my checking account."**（念 routing + account 两串数字）
5. 修地址（关键！卡和账单五周没到，地址八成有错）：
   > **"Can you verify the mailing address on file?"**
6. 补寄卡 + 绑邮箱：
   > **"Please send a replacement card, and add my email to the account for statements and alerts."**
